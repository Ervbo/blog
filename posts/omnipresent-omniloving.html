Omnipresent. Omniloving

Happy BSF day! We made it. Heck, even I made it.

I was doing my morning ritual the other day — Quiet Time, Bible study, and a physics lecture or two — and got thinking about how much I love God's attributes. Not only are they perfect, not only are His behaviors 100% tied to them, but they're all in perfect balance with one another. My favorite three, if I have to pick, are His Omnipotence, Omniscience, and Omnipresence.

All three of those are extremely hard for a person to imitate, especially consistently — anyone can act all-knowing or fully present for a little while. Well, I recently stumbled on a science video that proves God can be omnipresent, all of the time (link below). What a relief. Don't you love it when science proves something from the Bible so we can finally believe it? Imagine if science could prove God unconditionally, always loves us — Omniloving! Now that would be revolutionary. — Erv
