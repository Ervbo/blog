Ecclesiastes. Screensaver

Happy Labor Day to you all! What better way to celebrate Labor Day than by not doing any labor — well, actually, I am doing some labor. I'm writing a computer program in Python, the one language I never had the chance to learn, but with AI now, all programming feels pretty approachable.

The reason for the program: I recently watched, then listened to, a great video summary of Ecclesiastes. Of all the Exegetical summaries out there for each book of the Bible, I'd put Bible and Life and The Bible Project at the top of the list.

In general, I love Ecclesiastes. In one sense it's my favorite book of the Bible — that sense being its overall intellectual honesty. Anyone can read it and instantly see the big picture of life, how life should be lived, and the answers to life's biggest questions, without being deceived by what the world says about it. I believe it should be required reading for everyone before they graduate high school.

Here's the YouTube video — about an hour long. I liked it so much I wanted every word to lodge into my brain, which is the actual reason for the program. I wanted a screensaver that takes any text file and randomly displays quotes every 3–4 seconds in different spots on the screen. The beauty is I can feed it anything: one text file of quotes from favorite videos, one of favorite Bible verses, one of all the brilliant things Kamala Harris has said (currently empty). It works great — right now all the lines I loved from the video are bouncing around my screen.

I've posted the quotes to a Google Doc if anyone wants to read through them without running the program itself.

Can you believe we are just one week away from Exile & Return? What a fitting title for our study — and for me personally, if you think about it.

PS. I tried hosting the screensaver on the web through PythonAnywhere so it would work no matter what device someone's on — PC, Mac, iPad, Android — but ran into a display problem I haven't sorted out yet. More to come if I get it working. — Erv
