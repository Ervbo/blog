God Draws Near: The Series

Some threads on this Notebook grow one post at a time until, looking back, they turn out to be a series. This is the hub for the second one: God Draws Near — the story of a lovesick Father closing the distance, from the Garden to the city named The LORD Is There. Here is the whole thread in reading order. I will keep this page updated as it grows.

The series

  1. God Draws Near (Jun 4, 2028)
    Where it starts: my one-sentence summary of the whole Bible — a lovesick Father desperately looking for His children to bring them home — and the full story of God’s presence, era by era, in one table.
  2. God Draws Near. Part 2. (Jun 11, 2028)
    The golden calf, and the hardest sentence in Exodus: “I will not go with you.” Why the accurate word is not that God wanted to destroy His people — but that He must. On holiness, immutability, and a love with no greater.
  3. God Draws Near. Part 3. (Jun 18, 2028)
    The finale: God’s nearness in the wilderness and the Promised Land, and why the Temple was never the final step — the dwelling place was always headed for human hearts.
  4. Lovesick: The Sermon (Jul 2, 2028)
    The series became a sermon for home church — and the sermon carries the story across the threshold the trilogy stopped at: Immanuel, the torn veil, and home at last. Free teaching kit: manuscript, study guide, and slides.

Grown from the same soil

  • Read It in the Order It Happened (Jun 25, 2028)
    A one-year chronological reading plan — watch the whole story of God drawing near unfold in the order it happened.

If you are new here, start with God Draws Near and read forward. And if this thread keeps growing — He is not finished drawing near, after all — this page will always hold the map.
