Creation. Day 4

I am, honestly, overwhelmed. Speechless. I just watched two videos that pushed my brain over the edge. I might be certifiably insane now, but there are indications that this is more madness than insanity. Anyone who knows me knows how much I love math, physics, cosmology, and quantum mechanics. Anyone who knows me even a little better knows I love the Bible more. So when a video hits both of those at once, it lands like a one-two punch. I actually said “wow… Wow… WOW… WOW!” out loud after finishing them.

The first is worth a watch. The second one you must watch, start to finish, even the parts where the Hebrew gets a little dense:

  • First video
  • Second video — the must-watch, ~18 minutes

Genesis's fourth day of creation — the sun, moon, and stars being set in place — carries more in the original language than a casual reading in English lets on. Watching how the Hebrew text was actually built here reframed the whole passage for me, and it's the kind of thing worth setting aside a full evening to sit with rather than rushing through in a five-minute scroll.
