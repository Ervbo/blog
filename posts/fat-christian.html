Biblical March 4, 2027

A FAT Christian

Happy BSF day! Did you know there's an actual BSF song for Discussion days? Worth a listen if you haven't heard it.

Have you ever heard the quote, “It is doubtful whether God can bless a man greatly until He has hurt him deeply” — A.W. Tozer? I have, and I wish it weren't true for me, but it is. It puts me in mind of two verses that are also uncomfortably true of me: “Before I was afflicted, I went astray, but now I keep and honor Your word” (Ps 119:67), and “When God began killing them, they finally sought Him. They repented and took God seriously” (Ps 78:34).

I couldn't help but laugh at the notes for Lesson 1 this week: “However, God had plans for the flattened Ezekiel.” My first thought was, “hey, I resemble that remark.” Back in college I learned that God is looking for FAT Christians — Faithful, Available, Teachable. That's stuck with me ever since. Maybe a better version is FLAT — or, honestly, FAT FLAT. Either one works as a term of endearment next time we see each other. Hey FATTY! Hey FLATTY!

Like all of you, I love the BSF notes, and lately I've been taking the embedded discussion questions a lot more seriously. In case anyone needs a reminder, here's this week's set:

  • Have you ever felt desperate or deserted? Do you think God sees you, hears you, or helps you when you feel this way?
  • How does Ezekiel's vision open your eyes to God's unlimited power, rightful authority, and abiding presence?
  • What would such obedience bring to Ezekiel's life? Did God promise a smooth path for the exiled prophet?
  • How do you respond when you face surprising circumstances that leave you feeling stuck or hopeless?
  • How will you seek God's purpose in your unfulfilled aspirations?
  • What unanswered questions and unexpected course changes do you face today?
  • How will you trust God's bigger purposes?
  • How has God enlarged your understanding of who He is and humanity's accountability to Him?
  • Do you study the Bible not just to solve your problems but to gaze at God and learn about Him?
  • Life's challenges come into proper perspective when our view of God is big. How will you intentionally fasten your gaze on God and His glory today?
  • How have you experienced God's greatness?
  • Do you realize your position before Him?
  • If you are God's child by faith in Christ, God raises you to stand in His presence and fills you with His Spirit. What does this mean in your life today?
