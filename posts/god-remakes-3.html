God REmakes. Part 3.

Part 3 of the God REmakes series.

Five more words this time, and notice how they cluster: God revealing what was hidden, returning us to what we forgot, and waking, reviving, and re-strengthening what had gone dim. One of these — REAWAKEN — was among the questions I asked back in Part 1 (“What did He wake us to again? How were we sleeping?”). Here is the answer. Every verse below is now a link — click any reference to read it for yourself. That goes for the whole series from now on.

REVEAL

Definition
Make known information that was previously known only to a few, or that was meant to be kept a secret.

Take it to heart
God makes Himself, His will, humanity’s sin, and His saving purpose known through His Word, His prophets, and supremely through Christ.

Deuteronomy 29:29
God knows some things that He has not told us, and that is okay. But He has told us what we need to know so we can love Him and obey Him.

John 1:14–18
Jesus, who is God’s Son, came down from heaven and lived with people on earth. He showed us how much God loves us, always tells the truth, and gives us many good gifts. John the Baptist told everyone that Jesus was much greater than he was. Jesus helps us know what God is like because He knows God perfectly.

Romans 1:16–20
The Good News about Jesus is powerful because God uses it to save everyone who trusts Him. We cannot make ourselves right with God by being perfect; we are made right by believing in Jesus. God also made a beautiful world that shows He is real, powerful, and wise, so people should seek Him instead of choosing sin.

Hebrews 1:1–3
Long ago, God spoke to people through prophets. Now He has spoken to us through Jesus, His Son. Jesus shows us exactly what God is like, helped create the whole world, and keeps everything going by His powerful word. Jesus died to wash away our sins, then returned to heaven to rule beside God.

REVISIT

Definition
Visit again.

Take it to heart
God repeatedly returns His people to foundational truths, past mercies, covenant promises, and lessons they have neglected.

Deuteronomy 8:2
Remember how God took care of His people during their hard journey. He was teaching them to trust Him and obey Him.

Psalm 77:11–12
When you feel sad or worried, remember the wonderful things God has done. Thinking about His power can help you trust Him again.

2 Peter 1:12–15
Peter wanted Christians to keep remembering the true things they had learned about Jesus, even after Peter was gone.

Revelation 2:4–5
Jesus told people who had stopped loving Him as much as before to remember their first love, turn back to Him, and begin obeying Him again.

REVIVE

Definition
Give new life or energy to. Cause to regain consciousness.

Take it to heart
God restores spiritual life, joy, courage, worship, and obedience among people who have grown weak, discouraged, or spiritually cold.

Psalm 85:6
The writer asks God, “Please make us spiritually alive and joyful again, so we can be happy in You.”

Psalm 119:25, 37
The writer feels weak and close to giving up, so he asks God to give him new life and help him turn away from things that do not matter.

Habakkuk 3:2
Habakkuk remembers God’s amazing deeds and asks God to help His people again, while still showing them mercy.

Hosea 6:1–3
God’s people say, “Let’s go back to the Lord.” They trust that God can heal their hurts, help them grow strong again, and bless them like rain helps flowers grow.

REVITALIZE

Definition
Give new life or vigor to. Restore strength.

Take it to heart
God renews strength and fruitfulness through His Spirit, making weary people and discouraged communities alive for faithful service.

Isaiah 40:28–31
God made everything, and He never gets tired. When we feel weak, we can trust Him to give us new strength to keep going.

Ezekiel 37:1–14
God showed Ezekiel a valley full of dry bones. God brought the bones together and gave them breath and life to show that He could give hope and new life to His people when they thought everything was hopeless.

Ephesians 3:16
Paul prayed that God would make His people strong on the inside through the power of the Holy Spirit.

REAWAKEN

Definition
Awaken once again.

Take it to heart
God tells His people to wake up, be strong, and remember that He is rescuing them from their sadness and slavery.

Isaiah 52:1
God calls His people, like a city that has been asleep, to wake up, put on their beautiful clothes, and shake off the dust of their captivity — because He is setting them free.

Romans 13:11–14
It is time to wake up and live for Jesus. We should turn away from wrong choices and let Jesus help us do what is right.

Ephesians 5:8–14
Before we knew Jesus, it was like living in darkness. Now we belong to the light, so we should do good things, find out what pleases God, and shine His goodness for others to see.

Seven words down, over 120 to go. The map in Part 1 now shows which words have been covered — every covered word is a link. And the invitation stands: if a word on the map has started preaching to you, tell me in the Comments. I take requests.
