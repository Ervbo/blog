Pray for Cori. AI.

A friend asked me to pray for his daughter, Cori, who's going through a hard health battle right now. I wanted to bring something to that prayer that was actually grounded in what Scripture reveals about God's will for healing, not just well-meaning words. So I worked through it slowly — what does God actually promise here, and what doesn't He promise?

Three things seem clear from Scripture. God delights to heal and restore, both physically and spiritually (Jeremiah 33:6, Psalm 147:3, Psalm 103:3). He calls us to pray for the sick in faith, within the fellowship of His people, trusting Him to act (James 5:14–15). And all healing — whatever form it takes — is grounded in Christ's finished work, where "by his wounds we are healed" (Isaiah 53:5, 1 Peter 2:24), ultimately in forgiveness and resurrection life. So when I pray for healing, I'm not trying to twist God's arm. I'm bringing an honest desire under His revealed will and asking Him to be glorified in whatever He chooses to do.

Here's the prayer I settled on for Cori:

Father in heaven, I come to you in the name of Jesus, who bore our sins in his body on the cross so that we might die to sin and live for righteousness, and by whose wounds we are healed. I lift up Cori before your throne of grace and ask that you stretch out your hand to heal her.

Lord, you are the God who says, "Heal me, Lord, and I will be healed; save me and I will be saved, for you are the one I praise," and you promise to bring health and healing to your people. Would you, in your mercy, bring that healing to Cori's body — driving out disease, restoring what is damaged, and strengthening what is weak.

You are the One who heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds, and who sustains your children on their sickbed. I ask that you draw near to Cori now with your comforting presence, quiet her fears, lift her spirit, and surround her with your peace that passes understanding.

Holy Spirit, guide the doctors, nurses, and all who care for her with wisdom and skill. Let medications, treatments, and therapies be effective and well-tolerated, and protect her from complications or harm.

Above all, Father, I pray that your good and perfect will be done in Cori's life. If it would glorify your name, grant her full and speedy healing; if you, in your wisdom, choose a slower path, then fill her and her family with persevering faith, deep comfort, and a powerful sense of your nearness. Let this trial draw Cori and her loved ones closer to Jesus and not farther away.

We entrust Cori completely into your loving hands, confident that you are good, that you are able, and that you are with her now. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, amen.

If you're reading this and it's not too much to ask — would you take a minute and pray it for Cori too?
