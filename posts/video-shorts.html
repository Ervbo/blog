Happy Monday. Just got back from the dentist — no cavities, though at this rate I half expect my teeth to eventually turn into some kind of calcium-tungsten alloy from sheer age.

You can skip the next paragraph if you want and jump straight to the nine links below — but if you're curious, here's a bit of the longer story behind them.

I've spent the last sixteen years accumulating Christian content — books, sermons, notes — and I've been on a long journey trying to organize it all. It started with a simple witnessing observation: we tend to love Jesus the way we love a friend, a parent, an athlete, or a teacher — often less, honestly, and that gap has always bothered me. Somewhere along the way I realized my hardcopy Christian library, as valuable as it was, was basically unsearchable. So I used a service called 1dollarscan.com to OCR-scan and digitize the entire thing — which meant destroying the physical originals in the process, a decision I still think about. From there everything went to the cloud — Google Docs, Dropbox, OneDrive, iCloud — then I bought a NAS, then I taught myself Notion. At this point it's less about finding things and more about organizing what I already have a little better.
