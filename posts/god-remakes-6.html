God REmakes. Part 6.

Part 6 of the God REmakes series.

Six words this time, and I think of them as the recovery ward: what God does with the lost, the twisted, the barren, the wounded, and the dead. Two of these cards also debut a new section — Apply it — because somewhere around the twentieth word of this study I realized these are not just definitions. They are testimonies waiting to be claimed. Every reference is a link.

RECOVER

Definition
Get, or get back; recover the use of.

Take it to heart
God seeks, heals, restores, and brings back what was lost — lost sheep, prodigal sons, exiles, broken people, and ultimately a groaning creation.

Luke 15
Jesus told stories about things that were lost and then found: a sheep, a coin, and a son. God loves us so much that He looks for people who are far from Him, welcomes them home when they turn back, and celebrates when they are found.

Psalm 23:3
God helps us feel strong again inside and gently leads us on the right path.

Jeremiah 30:17
God promises to heal His people’s hurts and help them become well again.

Romans 8:18–25
Sometimes life is painful, and even the world feels broken. But God promises a wonderful future when He will free His children and creation from sin, sickness, death, and decay, so we can wait for Him with hope.

RECREATE

Definition
Produce or make another of.

Take it to heart
In Christ, a person becomes a new creation; at the end God brings a renewed creation, free from the curse and death.

Genesis 1:26–31
God made people to be like Him in special ways. He gave them a beautiful world to care for, blessed them, and said that everything He made was very good.

2 Corinthians 5:17
When someone trusts Jesus, God gives that person a new life. The old sinful way does not have to control them anymore.

Revelation 21:1–5
One day God will make a new heaven and a new earth. He will live with His people, wipe away every tear, and there will be no more death, sadness, crying, or pain.

RECTIFY

Definition
Correct, fix, improve, or make right something that is wrong or defective.

Take it to heart
God sets right what sin has twisted: He judges evil, vindicates the oppressed, forgives guilt, and establishes righteousness. The Bible’s own phrase for it is set right.

Psalm 89:14
God always rules in the right and fair way. He is full of love and always tells the truth.

Isaiah 1:16–18
God tells His people to stop doing wrong, learn to do good, help people who are treated badly, and ask Him to forgive their sins. God can wash away even very serious sins and make people clean.

Romans 3:21–26
Everyone has sinned, but God loves us and offers forgiveness as a gift. Jesus died for our sins so that everyone who trusts Him can be made right with God.

Revelation 19:11
Jesus will come as a mighty King. He is faithful and true, and He will judge everything in the perfectly right way.

RECULTIVATE

Definition
Prepare land for crops again; readapt what has gone wild.

Take it to heart
Humanity was made to cultivate and guard Eden; after the fall, the ground became cursed. Restoration includes fruitful land, healed creation, and faithful human stewardship.

Genesis 2:15
God gave Adam a wonderful garden to take care of and protect. God wants people to care for the world He made.

Genesis 3:17–19
After Adam disobeyed God, growing food became hard work. The ground would grow weeds and thorns, reminding people that sin had brought trouble into God’s good world.

Isaiah 35:1–2
God promises that dry, empty places will someday become beautiful and full of flowers, joy, and singing. God can bring new life where things seem sad or hopeless.

Romans 8:19–23
The whole world is waiting for God to fix what sin has broken. One day, God will free His children and His creation from sickness, death, and decay.

RECUPERATE

Definition
Get over an illness or shock. Restore to good health or strength.

Take it to heart
God heals wounded people and restores spiritual strength, joy, and health — personally and nationally. The Bible’s more direct words are heal and renew.

Psalm 23:3
God helps us feel strong again inside and lovingly shows us the right way to live.

Psalm 51:10–12
David asks God to forgive him, give him a clean heart, help him want to obey, and bring back the joy of knowing God.

Jeremiah 30:17
God promises to heal His people’s hurts and help them become well again.

Apply it
How has God helped you recuperate? From being dead in sin? From hurt or bitterness? From an illness? From a financial setback? From a tragedy?

RESUSCITATE

Definition
Cause to regain consciousness.

Take it to heart
God gives life where death reigns: Ezekiel’s dry bones, Jesus’ raising of the dead, and resurrection itself. For the final hope, resurrect is the more exact word.

Ezekiel 37:1–14
God showed Ezekiel dry bones that came together and lived again. This showed that even when God’s people felt hopeless, God could give them new life and bring them back to Him.

John 11:25–26, 43–44
Jesus said He is the One who gives life beyond death. Then He called Lazarus out of the tomb, showing that Jesus has power over death.

1 Corinthians 15:20–23
Jesus rose from the dead first. Everyone who belongs to Jesus can be sure that one day God will raise them from the dead too.

Apply it
How has God given you new life? When you were born again. After something died within you. When He gave you hope. When you stood up and went forward again.

Twenty-one words lit on the map — a sixth of the territory walked. And the recovery ward ends where every ward on this map ends: at an empty tomb. Requests open in the Comments; the map is long and the Author is patient.
