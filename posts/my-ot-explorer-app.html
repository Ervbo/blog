MY OT Explorer App

I've added a few more things to my OT Explorer app, one of which might genuinely be useful to you: a Wiersbe link in the footer. We should always be careful with Bible commentaries — there are so many, and most of them are excellent, but none of them replace the Holy Spirit or our own diligence (2 Timothy 2:15, Proverbs 2:1–5). Like most things in life, some resources are just stronger in certain areas than others. It's hard to beat the BSF process itself — reading daily, reflecting, capturing your own thoughts, discussing, taking notes, hearing the lecture — and within that, hard to beat the BSF notes. Spurgeon (the Prince of Preachers) and Lutzer are both favorites of mine, but Warren Wiersbe — the Pastor's Pastor — is who I turn to when I want to dig deeper into God's Word. So I added a link to all his books in the app's footer, along with a handful of other favorites:

  • Bible Infographics — word clouds and Bible Project posters for every book, with mind maps coming soon.
  • All Data (Google Sheets) — the raw data I've gathered on every significant person and event in the Bible: patriarchs, priests, judges, prophets, kings, kingdom, exile, and return. The timelines were the hardest part, but invaluable once filtered and graphed.
  • 1 Minute Answers — sixty-second videos answering many of Christianity's hardest questions.
  • 1 Minute Summaries — sixty-second video summaries of nearly every book in the Bible, a recent discovery I've found genuinely useful.
  • Bible Project — Tim and Jon, whom I've followed since their very first podcast. Tim is an excellent Old Testament theologian; Jon is gifted at explaining books of the Bible graphically.
  • Chapter Titles — titles for every chapter in the Bible, useful when starting a new study, found via Austin Bible Church.
  • ABC (Austin Bible Church) and GN Topics / GN Courses (Grace Notes) — deep resources, including actual online Bible courses and degree paths.
  • Bible.org — a teaching blog with thorough material, much of it from DTS graduates.
  • Wiersbe and Spurgeon — nothing more needs to be said.

You can get the app itself from the Play Store: bit.ly/Old-Testament-Explorer.
