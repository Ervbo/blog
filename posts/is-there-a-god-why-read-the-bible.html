Is There a God? Why Read the Bible?

I took two earlier, longer emails and boiled them down into something easier to read and easier to pass along to friends or family who might not want to wade through a wall of text.

The first is on a well-known commentator who argues, from science, that there can't be a God. I'll admit I like the way he thinks — he's articulate and clearly intelligent. But when it comes to God, he makes some real errors in logic. I turned his argument into a flowchart so you can try to spot them yourself: Is There a God?

The second is a summary of a John Piper message on why we need to be in God's Word constantly, and why His Word needs to be constantly in us. I turned the podcast into bullet points to make it easier to absorb: Why Read the Bible?
