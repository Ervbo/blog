Biblical July 25, 2027

Free Seminary Classes. DTS

How are you guys doing? I hope you all are enjoying your summer break from BSF. I'm anxious to get started with Exile & Return — we're less than two months away now. I still haven't decided if I should return to our group meetings in person, but rest assured, I'll be involved somehow. I love BSF. I love this group.

I'm doing okay. That's really the best I can ever hope for anymore. I did have a great birthday though — I visited my son and his family and got to play nonstop with my three grandchildren. Lately, though, I've once again retreated from life and am just doing the minimum to get by. I owe some of you an email response and I'll try to get to it soon. I'm normally a maniac in my Bible study, but I'm even doing just the bare minimum there too. I'm not even reading or listening to Spurgeon sermons. I say this to my own disgrace.

However, I tried to get back on the horse this morning by listening to Dallas Theological Seminary's free online courses. The one I'm doing now is called Bible Study Methods by Dr. Mark Bailey. I've completed 25–30 of these courses and all of them are worth your time — I can say that confidently since they're free. I love the policies within BSF that keep us from sharing our denominational preferences and steer us away from rabbit holes and controversial subjects (looking at you, US politics), so I think I'm safe here just recommending this DTS course. For me, I was raised on this exact Bible study method that Dr. Bailey presents — from about 46 years ago now. I'm well-traveled in Christian Land, in my ministries and in my studies, and after my tumor I added the internet to that scope. At no time have I ever seen anyone in Christian Land disagree on the method of Bible study presented here. I've heard a few disagree with some of the teachings out of DTS, Dispensationalism, and Calvinism, but in my 46 years with DTS I haven't seen them push those doctrines overtly. If digging into their Statement of Belief and finding traces of Calvinism freaks you out, don't throw the baby out with the bathwater — my favorite quote.

DTS Global Institute

I'll take this time to thank you all again for hanging in with me now that I'm a sociopath. The doctors have given up on helping me with my brain, but they can at least get my blood pressure and cholesterol down. If you're going to be a sociopath, it's apparently important to keep your cholesterol and blood pressure normal. Not sure why, but that's what they tell me. — Erv
