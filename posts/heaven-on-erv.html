Heaven on Erv

I was recently in a BSF discussion on Romans 1:18–32. One of the questions that came up was — how does God reveal Himself to the world? In what ways? For what reason(s)? I answered the question, but I explained that much of my answer contained what I had to learn after the effects of my brain tumor became obvious. Simply put — how does God interact with me if I am unable to go to church? Conversely, how do I interact with Him when I am unable to go to church? What I want to share here is my answer to both of those questions. Many of my blog posts I hope to get Comments on, but especially this one. I want to hear your thoughts. Do you agree? What could I do better?

Here I want to start with an ‘ideal’ answer to both questions. I did not start there. When I realized how hard it was to go to church, and that I honestly had to stop, I went more into a reactive mode: how can I recreate what I have lost? But in retrospect I wish I would have started with what was enabled or intended by God, versus what I ‘needed.’

I am going to try three approaches here. Answering the question from: 1) the perspective of heaven on earth — what it ideally would look like in our churches; 2) the perspective of heaven in my house — what it ideally would look like in my own personal Sanctuary; and 3) the perspective of heaven in my world — what it ideally would look like in my own personal World. For now, I am going to address 2): what it would look like in my own personal Sanctuary.

Let’s start here.

Heavenly realityPersonal expression at home
God’s presenceA regular quiet place where you come consciously before God — Bible, chair, journal, perhaps a cross, artwork, or a verse that helps you remember whose presence you are entering.
Worship and praiseWorship music, singing aloud, reading or singing Psalms, kneeling, raising hands, speaking praise, or simply sitting in reverent silence. Scripture calls God’s people to sing and make melody to the Lord from the heart (Ephesians 5:19).
Truth revealedDaily Bible reading, my OICA study method, memorizing key passages, listening to faithful teaching, and studying Christian biographies that show truth lived out.
Prayer and intercessionA deliberate prayer time using my PLASTIC pattern: entering God’s Presence first, and from there — Listening, Adoration, Supplication, Thanksgiving, Intercession, and Confession. This reflects heaven’s concern with God’s will, worship, and the prayers of the saints (Revelation 5:8).
Joy and celebrationA special meal, a favorite drink, beautiful music, time outdoors, laughter, creative work, or a ‘thanksgiving list’ — received not as escape but as gifts from God.
Service and purposeWriting a biblical summary, developing a study tool, building my Bible app, preparing material for this blog, or creating something for my grandchildren. My house becomes a place from which blessing goes outward.

That is my Sanctuary so far — or at least the blueprint I am building toward. Parts 1) and 3) — heaven in our churches, and heaven in my World — will come in future posts. But now, especially on this one, I want your thoughts. Do you agree? What would you add? What could I do better? What does your sanctuary look like? The Comments below are open — and newly renovated. 😉
