Exile & Return

The lesson e-book for Exile & Return finally landed in my inbox, and I downloaded it the second it did. Thirty lessons this year, working through Ezra, Nehemiah, Daniel, Esther, Haggai, Zechariah, and Malachi — basically the whole story of Israel's exile and the long, uneven road back.

I skimmed ahead instead of pacing myself, which I always say I won't do and always do anyway. A few things already have my attention before we even get to Lesson 1:

  • Belshazzar's writing on the wall in Daniel 5 — MENE, MENE, TEKEL, PARSIN — still one of the most cinematic scenes in the whole Bible. God interrupting a party with an audit.
  • Esther's courage in going before the king uninvited, “if I perish, I perish” (Esth 4:16), which never stops being a gut-punch of a verse.
  • Ezekiel's valley of dry bones (Ezek 37) — not technically in this year's book list, but it's the same exile-era hope this whole study seems to be circling: God rebuilding what looked permanently dead.

There's something fitting about studying exile and return the same year I've been thinking so much about my own version of exile — from a normal life, from being able to keep up the way I used to. I'm looking forward to seeing where the parallel goes.

Study starts this week. Let's go. — Erv
