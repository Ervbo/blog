One Nation Under God

A letter to my BSF Brothers · April 15, 2025 · part of Letters to the Brothers.

I want to praise God with all of you about the Easter message I am hearing from the White House! I certainly don’t want to get into any political discussions because even when my brain was normal (or as normal as it could get) they could be brutal and easily get out of hand. I have been praying more for our country and our politicians now than at any other time in my life. Regularly, for 10 years at least. If there are those of you who do not like Trump, I get it. I did not like him either for the longest time. But when you read what he has recently posted up for the world to see, consider Philippians 1:15–18:

“It’s true that some are preaching out of jealousy and rivalry. But others preach about Christ with pure motives. They preach because they love me, for they know I have been appointed to defend the Good News. Those others do not have pure motives as they preach about Christ. They preach with selfish ambition, not sincerely, intending to make my chains more painful to me. But that doesn’t matter. Whether their motives are false or genuine, the message about Christ is being preached either way, so I rejoice. And I will continue to rejoice.” (Philippians 1:15–18, NLT)

In all honesty when I first memorized this passage, I had a huge problem with it. Even now I usually don’t think about it often and never share it with anyone. But in this case, I think it applies. Whether you love or hate Trump let’s at least “rejoice that the message about Christ is being preached.” Whether by pure motives or not. See? I could barely even type that last sentence 😕 Let us continue to pray for our country!

“Blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD” (Psalm 33:12).

The message itself: the Presidential Message on Holy Week 2025, and CBN’s coverage of the declaration and the White House Easter service. — Erv
