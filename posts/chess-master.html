PSA — Personal Show of Affection, or Public Service Announcement? I just noticed NotebookLM.google.com added a new feature. I already loved their Podcast option, but now there's a Video Overview option too. Give it a try — here's how:

  1. Go to Paper Back Bible and choose a book of the Bible you're interested in. I picked Ezekiel.
  2. Either right-click Download PDF and copy the link address, or just click through to download the PDF normally.
  1. Go back to NotebookLM.google.com and click Create new notebook.
  1. Paste the link you copied, or drag/upload the file you downloaded.
  2. Click the Video Overview option in the Studio panel.
  1. After about five minutes, you should get something like this.

Not a bad way to get a running start on a book before BSF picks it apart line by line tomorrow.
