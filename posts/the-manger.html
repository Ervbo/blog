The Manger. No Other Way.

Even though this post appears to be before my post Heaven or Hell. Your Choice., I wrote it afterward. One of the arguments I made in that post — “If there were any other solution than what Christ, His only begotten Son, endured, don’t you think He would have done it?” — has been lingering with me. In that post I followed it up with the agony Jesus Himself endured in the Garden of Gethsemane, as His own disciples fell asleep, leaving Him alone to face what was about to come. Twice He begged God the Father to “let this cup of suffering be taken away from Me,” while His soul was “crushed with grief to the point of death” (Matthew 26:38–39). To me that is proof positive that there was no other way. Because Jesus asked God His Father if there were any other way. If God, who is omniscient, had no other option, how could there be any other way? Imagine if your own son had to die — in the cruelest death imaginable — and begged you to find any other way. What would you do?

But is this the first time we as readers of the Bible finally understand this? I can think of many other times Jesus addressed this same question while He was alive on earth. But I want to go back to the beginning. When God put on flesh and was born in a manger.

What do we see when Christ was born? What do we see in that manger? His birth had previously been announced; the ‘good news’ was announced; the angel had appeared to the shepherds, the glory of the Lord shone around them, and an enormous company of the heavenly host was there praising God (Luke 2:8–14). The wise men from the east arrive and ask, “Where is the newborn king of the Jews? We saw His star as it rose, and we have come to worship Him” (Matthew 2:1–2). The king asks the Jewish priests, “Where is the Messiah supposed to be born?” (Matthew 2:4). I am sure I have left out some other important events, but my point is: a lot is going on! This is a unique, pivotal moment in the history of all eternity! And I think, with all the drama and the excitement, we might miss the fact that the question Jesus asked His Father in Gethsemane was being answered then as well. Only quietly. The answer was the same: “No. There is no other way.”

What was the name that Jesus was given before His birth? “God with us” (Matthew 1:23)… “He will save His people from their sins” (Matthew 1:21). “When the right time came, God sent his Son, born of a woman” (Galatians 4:4), and “a body you have prepared for me” (Hebrews 10:5). I must make a comment here with my Engineer hat on. I have designed and built many things in my life. Through my education and training, I am an expert in that area. The body that Jesus was given was designed exactly for this rescue mission. Simply put: it was built for the job. It met all the requirements.

I have written a post, coming in the new year, where I go into detail about 4 times in my life I had to go through something terrible. The operative word is through. There was no other way. This was the moment. This was the solution. This was the only way. What nobody else saw that night, the Father did. And it was then — it was there — that He answered His Son’s pleading question: “If there is any other way, please let this cup of suffering be taken away from Me.”

The answer was lying in the manger. Merry Christmas.
