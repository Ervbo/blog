God REmakes. Part 5.

Part 5 of the God REmakes series.

Five words this time, and they arrived in formation: RECONFIGURE, RECONFIRM, RECONNECT, RECONCILE, RECONSTRUCT. The recon- squad. Together they tell the story in order: God reshapes His people, keeps confirming His promises, restores the connection sin severed, makes peace through the cross, and rebuilds what was ruined — all the way to a city with His name on it. Every reference is a link.

RECONFIGURE

Definition
Arrange or set up in a new way.

Take it to heart
The Holy Spirit reshapes believers into Christ’s likeness; God’s kingdom reverses the world’s fallen values and patterns. The Bible’s own word for it is transform.

Romans 12:2
Do not copy the wrong things people around you do. Let God teach you to think in a new way so you can learn what He wants you to do.

2 Corinthians 3:18
As we look to Jesus and follow Him, God slowly changes us to become more like Him.

Philippians 3:20–21
Christians belong to heaven, and we are waiting for Jesus to come back. One day He will change our weak bodies to be wonderful and strong like His risen body.

RECONFIRM

Definition
Establish or strengthen as with new evidence or facts. Confirm again.

Take it to heart
God repeatedly confirms His covenant promises — to Abraham, to Israel, to David, and finally in Christ.

Genesis 17:1–8
God promised Abraham that he would have a very large family. God changed Abram’s name to Abraham and promised to always be the God of Abraham and his children.

2 Samuel 7:12–16
God promised King David that someone from his family would always be king. This promise ultimately points to Jesus, the forever King.

Luke 1:68–73
Zechariah praised God because He was sending Jesus, the Savior, just as He had promised long ago to Abraham, David, and the prophets.

2 Corinthians 1:20
Every promise God has made is kept through Jesus. We can trust God because He always does what He says.

RECONNECT

Definition
Join together or make a link between two or more pieces. Connect again.

Take it to heart
Sin separates humanity from God; Christ brings access, peace, and restored fellowship. (If this word sounds familiar, it should — it is the whole story of my God Draws Near series.)

Genesis 3:22–24
After Adam and Eve disobeyed God, they had to leave the beautiful Garden of Eden. God guarded the way to the tree of life so they would not live forever in their sinful condition.

Isaiah 59:2
Sin separates people from God, like a wall that blocks friendship with Him. But God can forgive us and bring us close to Him again.

Romans 5:1–2
When we trust Jesus, He makes us right with God. We can have peace with God now and joyful hope for the future.

Ephesians 2:18
Because of what Jesus did, everyone who believes in Him can come to God the Father through the Holy Spirit.

RECONCILE

Definition
Restore friendly relations after a disagreement or conflict. Bring into consonance or accord.

Take it to heart
This is a major New Testament word. Through Christ’s cross, God makes peace with believing people and sets in motion the reconciliation of all things in heaven and earth.

Romans 5:10–11
Even when we were far from God, Jesus died to bring us back to Him. Now we can be happy because Jesus has made us God’s friends.

2 Corinthians 5:18–21
God sent Jesus to take the punishment for our sins so we could be forgiven and become close to God. Then God asks us to help others learn that they can become His friends too.

Colossians 1:19–22
Jesus shows us exactly what God is like. By dying on the cross, Jesus made peace between God and people, bringing those who trust Him close to God.

RECONSTRUCT

Definition
Restore; return to its original or usable and functioning condition. Build or construct again.

Take it to heart
Jerusalem and the temple were rebuilt after exile; the prophets use rebuilding imagery for spiritual restoration. Christ builds His church, and the final goal is the New Jerusalem.

Ezra 1–6
God helped His people return home after they had been taken far away. Even when rebuilding was hard, God helped them rebuild His temple so they could worship Him again.

Nehemiah 1–6
Nehemiah was sad because Jerusalem’s wall was broken down. He prayed to God, led the people to work together, and God helped them rebuild the wall even though enemies tried to stop them.

Amos 9:11–15
God promised that He would repair what was broken, bring His people home, help them rebuild ruined cities, and give them a safe, fruitful place to live.

Matthew 16:18
Jesus said He would build His church — His family of people who believe in Him — and nothing, not even death, could defeat it.

Revelation 21:1–5
One day God will make a new heaven and a new earth. God will live with His people, wipe away every tear, and there will be no more death, sadness, crying, or pain.

Fifteen words lit on the map. Next week: the recovery ward — six words about what God does with the lost, the sick, and the dead. Requests still open in the Comments.
