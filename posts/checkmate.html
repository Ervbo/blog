Checkmate

Resending a thought I shared with you guys a couple weeks ago — I forgot to include the video clip that went with it the first time.

Picture God as a Master Chess player in the cosmic war with Satan. Sometimes He allows Himself to lose a pawn. Sometimes a knight, a bishop — sometimes even the queen. My own frontal lobe was my queen, if we're being honest about the brain tumor. But a master player doesn't lose pieces carelessly. Every loss is strategic, calculated toward one outcome: checkmating Satan in the end.

We just need to understand that and stop accusing God of not caring about our losses and pain. None of it is ever wasted or careless in God's chess game.
