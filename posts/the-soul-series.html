The Soul: The Series

Some threads on this Notebook grow one post at a time until, looking back, they turn out to be a series. This is the hub for the longest one: the Soul. It began with a simple observation — you are an eternal living soul, and nothing else about your life matters as much — and kept unfolding from there. Here is the whole thread in reading order. I will keep this page updated as it grows.

The series

  1. The Soul (Mar 19, 2028)
    Where it starts: as an eternal soul you only have two decisions to make in your entire life — where your soul spends eternity, and in what condition. With a flowchart of the two forks: the one you answer once, and the one that never stops.
  2. Your Soul. The 3 Things. (Apr 30, 2028)
    I asked what the two-decision framework might be missing, and the answer added a third thing: your motives. Not just whose kingdom you build — why you build it.
  3. Your Rewards in Heaven. Not Intel. (May 7, 2028)
    Intel’s Ranking and Rating rewarded what you did alone, never what you did for the team. I once proposed fixing that with two raises. God already does — your soul’s reward includes what you did for and with the Body of Christ.
  4. Soul. Spurgeon. (May 14, 2028)
    The finale: the Prince of Preachers runs the ultimate audit on Mark 8:36 — profit, loss, and the human soul — as an interactive podcast plus seventeen slides.
  5. The Journey of the Soul (May 28, 2028)
    The epilogue: an allegory in eight panels, inspired by The Picture of Dorian Gray — but here the portrait is real. Two individuals, two thrones, and the soul each life prepares.

The word study behind it

Underneath the whole series is one word that is easy to use casually and rarely studied: soul. I traced every occurrence across the Hebrew Old Testament and the Greek New Testament, split into companion pages:

  • The Soul: Old Testament Word Study (companion)
    Every Hebrew word behind “soul,” led by nephesh.
  • The Soul: New Testament Word Study (companion)
    The Greek side, led by psychē.
  • The Soul: References (companion)
    The full reference list for the study.

A prelude, years earlier

  • Leanness in your soul (Sep 2, 2026)
    Written long before the series had a name — on Psalm 106:15, and what it costs a soul to get what it insists on.

If you are new here, start with The Soul and read forward. And if the thread keeps growing — and I suspect it will — this page will always hold the map.
