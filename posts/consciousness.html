Consciousness

I just watched a video of Roger Penrose — a Nobel laureate — talking about consciousness from a technical, analytical angle, and his conclusion startled me. Startled me and also didn't, because my own thinking has been circling that same space for a long time. I'm no Penrose, but he states something around the eight- or nine-minute mark that I'm going to be chewing on for a long while.

I've longed for a Theory of Everything since my college days — the link between Relativity and Quantum Mechanics that's eluded physics for a century. Penrose's answer: consciousness is that link. That one sentence rearranged something in my head. It's the first framework I've heard that doesn't just make room for God in the equation — it puts Him at the center of it.

I used AI to turn the lecture into a podcast format so I could send it to friends and family more easily — something they could actually sit with and even ask questions of, as if it were a live conversation. If you'd rather hear Penrose's own words, the original ~12-minute lecture is here on YouTube.
