Psalm 91

While working through this week's lesson, I decided to try something a little different: I generated an image using only the language of Psalm 91 as the prompt. There's so much packed into this psalm about how thoroughly God cares for us that I thought seeing it might help me absorb more of it than reading alone had.

Psalm 91 stacks image after image of protection — shelter of the Most High, shadow of the Almighty, refuge and fortress, wings you can take cover under, a shield of faithfulness. It's easy to read past all of that quickly because the words are familiar. Slowing down to actually picture it did something reading alone hadn't — it made the promises feel less like a list and more like a place I could actually stand.
