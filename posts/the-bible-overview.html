The Bible. Overview.

I recently finished a couple of pet projects that have been on my mind for a while. The first is an overview of the entire Bible, book by book, with a timeline attached. The second is every place in Scripture where Jesus appears or is presented as God — not just the Gospels, where He states it plainly and people respond to Him as God, but the Old Testament before His incarnation and in heaven now, and when He returns.
