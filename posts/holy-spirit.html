Holy Spirit

A letter to my BSF Brothers · April 16, 2025 · part of Letters to the Brothers.

Blessed Spy Wednesday to you all! Or should I say cursed Spy Wednesday? I am not big into rituals, but I certainly love to celebrate Christmas and Easter. Not just on those ‘special’ days but always. I know you have heard me say many times I have tried my best to follow my college roommate’s advice — live every day as though Christ died yesterday, rose today and is coming tomorrow. I need to fit ‘was born’ in there as well 😉. Rumor has it that Spy Wednesday is the day in Passion Week we pause and remember the betrayal of our Lord by Judas. Certainly nothing to celebrate. Except that I have been like Judas at times and denied my Lord in one way or another — but like Peter, not Judas, went crawling back to Him to receive His forgiveness. Maybe I can celebrate that I am a forgiven Judas on this day? Maybe that’s a stretch? Still, I hope and pray for us all that we will take time and sit (I prefer lay) before the Lord and recall all that He went through this week — and His entire life, really — for us… only to rise in glorious triumph Sunday!

One of the Brothers wrote recommending Philip Yancey’s What’s So Amazing About Grace?, and it elicited a response from me: YES! That book is a life-changer! I have read all of Yancey’s books. He is a heavy hitter and is willing to tackle the most difficult subjects (Where Is God When It Hurts?, Disappointment with God, Prayer: Does It Make Any Difference?, What Good Is God?). I would add The Jesus I Never Knew to the recommendation.

While I have you here, I need to share a new insight God gave me last night. You guys know I am a fanatic about Charles Spurgeon, and I am still on a journey to read his 3,000+ sermons before I die. You also know how I have wrestled with whether someone can truly be saved if they don’t believe in the Trinity — specifically that Jesus is God, and even that the Holy Spirit is God. There are many reasons to believe one is not. For example, if you believe Jesus was an angel, how does the death of an angel take away our sin? How does an angel become flesh? (Without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness of sin.) The verse that has stood out to me is John 8:24 — unless you believe I AM you will die in your sins. Remember who Jesus was talking to and what I AM meant to them. A fun thing to do is look up this word in the LXX. It will blow your mind!

Anyway, I was rereading The Personality of the Holy Spirit last night — Spurgeon’s Sermon #4. A couple things hit me out of nowhere. Basically, the Prince of Preachers is saying that we are not saved — our baptism is a farce — if we do not believe the Holy Spirit is God. BOOM.

I will say that I have held back on being too dogmatic about this, because I am pretty sure if you had asked me when I was saved — is Jesus God? Is the Holy Spirit God? — I would not even have known what you were talking about. Yet I am absolutely certain I was saved in that moment. My life was noticeably different as I walked home from high school and rushed in the door to tell my parents I was saved! In my mind God holds us accountable for what we know and what He has revealed to us. I read the entire NT in about a month afterwards and I believe I would have answered — yes, Jesus is ‘probably’ God. But I was super confused about what the Holy Spirit even was! 😕 Soon enough it all made sense. I guess it is similar to the thief on the cross. All he knew was Jesus did not deserve to die for any sin; He was going to heaven. Remember me. — Erv
