Heaven

I have been thinking a lot about heaven lately. Probably a normal thing to do if you're 68 years old and have had a life-threatening brain tumor. What is the first thing that comes to your mind when you think of heaven? For me it is — Jesus on the throne, beauty, perfection, streets of gold, the heavenly host, worship, singing, declarations of praise, peace, rest, no more tears, no more pain, infinity, many mansions, and a place prepared for me. There are even more attributes, but these are the ones that initially come to mind.

But I have to ask myself — is this where I will be forever? Why will there be a new heaven and earth then? Is it in the clouds? Is it the third heaven Paul mentioned? What will I be doing there?

Have I been prepared to do something there by my time on earth? What will my spiritual body be like? Does my soul exist somewhere else before it is united with my spiritual body? Is heaven just another name for the new Jerusalem? When the New Jerusalem descends on earth, is that the new heaven? The combination of heaven and earth?

What does it mean to be a kingdom of priests there? To who? Over who? Why will I judge angels? Will I recognize everyone? Will I have all of my memories from earth?

When does the new earth take place? Is it a totally new earth or a perfectly restored earth? Will I be able to move upon it and interact with it in a similar way that I did in my earthly body? How does it compare to the Garden of Eden? Is it a restoration of God's original plan or a new reinvention of it?

Before I do the research I want to share my initial thoughts and then correct them afterwards. I believe that the idea of heaven being in the clouds somewhere and us being there forever praising God is not entirely correct. It is incomplete.

Right now I am reflecting on my entire life on earth. It is a mix of beautiful moments, moments of joy and exaltation, but also terrible moments, moments of pain and despair. In both of those I see clearly the conflict between God and evil.

No one denies the great beauty of our world, our universe. Can you imagine living here if there was never any sin to ruin it? Hold that thought. No one denies the incredible ugliness of our world, our universe. Can you imagine living here if there were no God to restrain it? Hold that thought as well.
