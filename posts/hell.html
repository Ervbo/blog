Hell

Brothers, Bible Buddies — happy birthday to me! Three quick things.

I was reading a book collecting all the answers John Piper has given over the years to Bible questions people asked him. To my surprise, the first ~50 pages were all questions on how and why to read the Bible — and I thought his answers were fantastic. Since I know each of you love the Bible as I do, I think you'll enjoy them too. Beyond that, they're genuinely useful the next time a friend or loved one asks you “why do you love the Bible so much?” or “why do you do BSF every year?”

I've also been thinking about heaven a lot lately — and then, naturally, a little about hell. My thoughts here are shaped heavily by C.S. Lewis and by a book from Josh Butler, but here's a small sliver of how I've come to see it.

Imagine St. Peter greets you at death and asks, “Do you want to go to Heaven or Hell?” You think about it and ask, “What's the difference?” He replies: “In Hell, you drink Coca-Cola for eternity. In Heaven, you drink water for eternity.”

Which do you choose? In this simplified picture, that's one key difference between the two — walking with God, or not. One is pleasurable but never actually satisfying. The other is satisfying, and becomes pleasurable.
