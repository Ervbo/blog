What Question Is God Asking You Right Now?

Do any of these questions stand out to you, for any reason? Could God be asking you one of them right now? What question has He been putting to you lately — whether it's on this list or not? — Erv

I asked AI to pull together every question God asks in the Old Testament and every question Jesus asks in the New Testament, then trimmed the list down to the ones that hit hardest. Here it is.

Questions God asks in the Old Testament:

  • “Where have you come from?” (to Satan)
  • “Have you noticed my servant Job?” (to Satan)
  • “How long will you refuse to submit to me?” (to Pharaoh)
  • “Is there any limit to my power?” (to Moses)
  • “How long will these people reject me?” (to Moses)
  • “Will they never believe me, even after all the miraculous signs I have done among them?” (to Moses)
  • “Who is this that questions my wisdom with such ignorant words?” (to Job, from the whirlwind)
  • “Where were you when I laid the foundations of the earth?” (to Job)
  • “Do you realize the extent of the earth?” (to Job)
  • “Where does the light come from, and where does the darkness go?” (to Job)
  • “Have you visited the treasuries of the snow?” (to Job)
  • “Does the rain have a father? Where does dew come from? Who is the mother of the ice?” (to Job)
  • “Can you hold back the movements of the stars? Are you able to restrain the Pleiades or Orion?” (to Job)
  • “Do you know the laws of the universe and how God rules the earth?” (to Job)
  • “Do you still want to argue with the Almighty? You are God's critic, but do you have the answers?” (to Job)

Questions Jesus asks in the New Testament:

  • “If you love only those who love you, what good is that?”
  • “You parents — if your children ask for a loaf of bread, do you give them a stone instead? Or if they ask for a fish, do you give them a snake?”
  • “Who do people say that the Son of Man is?” (to the disciples)
  • “Who do you say I am?” (to the disciples)
  • “Haven't you read the Scriptures?”
  • “Are you able to drink from the bitter cup of sorrow I am about to drink?” (to James and John)
  • “Whose picture and title are stamped on it?” (about the Roman coin)
  • “What do you think about the Messiah? Whose son is he?” (to the Pharisees)
  • “How can Satan cast out Satan? And if Satan is fighting against himself, how can he stand?”
  • “Who is my mother? Who are my brothers?”
  • “Won't you ever learn or understand? Are your hearts too hard to take it in?” (to the disciples)
  • “Where is your faith?” (to the disciples)
  • “What does the law of Moses say? How do you read it?” (to the expert in religious law)
  • “Why is it that the Messiah is said to be the son of David?”
  • “You are a respected Jewish teacher, and yet you don't understand these things?” (to Nicodemus)
  • “Are you going to leave, too?” (to the Twelve)
  • “Where are your accusers? Didn't even one of them condemn you?” (to the woman caught in adultery)
  • “For which one of these good deeds are you killing me?” (to the Jewish leaders)
  • “But when I, the Son of Man, return, how many will I find who have faith?”

— Erv
