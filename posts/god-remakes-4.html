God REmakes. Part 4.

Part 4 of the God REmakes series.

Three words this time, and the first one is the heavyweight of the whole map. Some of these re- words are borrowed pictures — the Bible never says “recharge,” but it describes the thing perfectly. REGENERATE is different: this is a word the church has used for the actual miracle at the center of salvation. As always, every reference is a link — click and read.

REGENERATE

Definition
Renew; reestablish on a new, usually improved, basis. Make new or like new. Reformed spiritually or morally.

Take it to heart
God gives new spiritual life through the Holy Spirit — new birth, a new heart, and renewal. This is a major theological word.

John 3:3–8
Jesus told Nicodemus that everyone needs to be born a second time — not from a mother, but from God’s Spirit. We cannot do this for ourselves, just like we did not make ourselves be born the first time. The Spirit gives this new life like the wind: we cannot see where it comes from, but we can see what it does. (I wrote a whole post on this one: Born Again.)

Titus 3:4–7
God saved us because He is kind and loves us, not because we were good enough. He washes away our sin, gives us new life through the Holy Spirit, and makes us His children who will live with Him forever.

Ezekiel 36:25–27
God promises to wash away His people’s sin, give them soft and willing hearts instead of stubborn hearts, and place His Spirit within them to help them obey Him.

1 Peter 1:3, 23
Because Jesus rose from the dead, God gives us a living hope. When we trust Jesus, God gives us new life that lasts forever through His living Word.

RECHARGE

Definition
Load anew. Of a person: revive to full strength after hard work or exertion.

Take it to heart
God renews weary people by His presence, His Spirit, and hope. The Bible never uses this modern word — but it describes the experience exactly.

Isaiah 40:28–31
God made the whole world, and He never gets tired or runs out of strength. When we feel weak, tired, or like giving up, we can trust God to help us and give us strength to keep going.

Psalm 23:3
God helps us feel strong again inside and shows us the right way to live.

Ephesians 3:16
Paul prayed that God would make His people strong in their hearts through the Holy Spirit.

RECLASSIFY

Definition
Classify anew; change the previous classification.

Take it to heart
In Christ, people receive a new identity: alienated people become God’s children, enemies become members of His household, and the unclean are declared clean.

John 1:12–13
Everyone who believes in Jesus and welcomes Him becomes part of God’s family. God gives them a new kind of life that comes from Him.

2 Corinthians 5:17
When someone belongs to Jesus, God begins a new life in that person. The old sinful way does not have to control them anymore.

Ephesians 2:11–22
Jesus brings people close to God and helps people who were separated become one family. Because of Jesus, we can all come to God and belong in His home.

1 Peter 2:9–10
God chose His people to belong to Him. He brought them out of darkness into His wonderful light so they can show others how good He is.

Ten words lit on the map, well over a hundred to go. And notice what these three make together: God gives you new life (regenerate), new strength (recharge), and a new name on the file (reclassify). The whole gospel in one column of the dictionary. Requests still open in the Comments.
