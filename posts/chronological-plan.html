Read It in the Order It Happened

The Bible’s books are arranged by type — law, history, poetry, prophets, gospels, letters — not by when things happened. Reading it chronologically, in story order, is a completely different experience: Job in the middle of Genesis, the Psalms landing inside David’s life as he lives it, the prophets speaking into the exact kings and crises they addressed. If you have read the Bible cover to cover before, I highly recommend a chronological pass. The whole story of God drawing near unfolds in order.
